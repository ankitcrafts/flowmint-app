# 💎 FlowMint: Business Model Canvas

## 1. 🎯 Customer Segments
- **Gen-Z and Millennials (India):** Tech-savvy users looking for a modern, premium financial management experience.
- **Credit Builders:** Users who want to improve their credit scores, track them, and simulate future financial decisions.
- **Goal-Oriented Savers:** Individuals aiming to save for specific lifestyle goals (e.g., travel, gadgets) with AI guidance.
- **Loan Seekers:** Users looking for pre-approved loans, credit cards, or borrowing opportunities from trusted NBFCs/Banks.

## 2. 🎁 Value Propositions
- **Centralized "Cyber-Finance" Hub:** A singular financial command center (super-app) combining banking, credit, and lifestyle tracking in a high-fidelity glassmorphic UI.
- **AI-Powered Wealth Management:** Groq (Llama 3.3) and Gemini AI providing personalized spending blueprints, saving plans, and real-time conversational financial advisory.
- **Financial Simulation & Education:** Interactive tools like the "What-If" credit simulator to help users predict the impact of their financial choices.
- **Automated Tracking & Reminders:** Expense tagging, anomaly detection, EMI stress meter, and bill reminders so users never miss a payment.
- **Eco-Impact Tracking:** Tracking carbon footprints and sustainability scores based on spending habits.

## 3. 📱 Channels
- **Mobile Application:** Built with Capacitor for Android (apk) and ready for iOS via App Stores.
- **Web App / PWA:** Direct browser access via desktop and mobile web with Service Worker auto-updates.
- **Organic & Social:** Targeting tech-first demographics via social media, financial literacy content, and community sharing loops.

## 4. 🤝 Customer Relationships
- **Automated AI Assistant:** The witty, Gen-Z styled "FlowMint AI" acting as a direct 24/7 personal finance advisor.
- **Self-Service Personalization:** Deep profile customization (vibe, avatar, GPS tracking) making the app feel individually tailored to the user.
- **Trust & Security:** On-device biometric vaults, AES-GCM encryption, and local data protection to build high user trust without relying on 3rd parties.
- **Gamification (Future Phase):** Points, badges, and the FlowCoins reward system to actively retain user engagement.

## 5. 💰 Revenue Streams
- **Lead Generation & Affiliate Fees:** Commission from NBFCs/Banks through the Loan Marketplace (FlowLend) when users convert on credit or loan offers.
- **Premium Subscription (FlowMint Black):** Subscription fees for advanced AI features, lower transaction fees, and exclusive experiences.
- **Interchange Fees (Future Phase):** Transaction fees via FlowPay (UPI/Payments) or co-branded credit cards.

## 6. 🔑 Key Activities
- **Software Development & UI/UX Design:** Continuous maintenance of the React/Supabase application, integrating new API endpoints, and polishing the rich UI.
- **AI Model Tuning & Prompt Engineering:** Refining the Groq & Gemini prompts for hyper-accurate, safe financial insights.
- **Partnership Acquisition:** Onboarding new banks, NBFCs, and financial institutions to expand the available products in the Loan Marketplace.
- **Security & Compliance Auditing:** Maintaining strict adherence to data protection (DPDP Act), RBI guidelines, and encryption standards.

## 7. 📦 Key Resources
- **Proprietary Technology Stack:** React 19, Express backend, and Supabase configured for high scale and strict Row Level Security (RLS).
- **AI Capabilities:** Connected infrastructure with Groq (Llama) and Gemini models.
- **Brand & Aesthetics:** The distinctive premium "Cyber-Finance" IP, glassmorphism design system, and custom animations.
- **Engineering Talent:** Developers, UI/UX designers, and product managers.
- **Secure Data:** User financial data, behavioral trends, and geographical context aggregated strictly to provide deep intelligence.

## 8. 🔗 Key Partnerships
- **Lending & Financial Partners:** Banks and NBFCs (HDFC, Bajaj Finserv, Muthoot, ICICI).
- **Credit Bureaus & Aggregators:** Experian/CIBIL (via Apify) and future Account Aggregators (AA).
- **Core Technology Providers:** Supabase (Database), Groq/Google (AI), App Stores (Apple/Google).
- **Identity Verification Providers:** Digio/Surepass (For Aadhaar/PAN KYC) and OpenStreetMap/Nominatim (Geocoding).
- **Payment Gateways:** Razorpay/PayU (Future UPI/bill integrations).

## 9. 💸 Cost Structure
- **Cloud Infrastructure & AI APIs:** Execution costs for Supabase (DB), Render (backend hosting), Groq (LLM), and Google Gemini APIs.
- **Integration Fees:** Per-call API costs for credit retrieval (Apify), KYC providers, and SMS OTP providers (Twilio/MSG91).
- **Development & Payroll:** Core overhead for developers, designers, and continuous operations.
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):** Marketing efforts, promotional incentives, and user acquisition campaigns.
